A warm, funny one-image letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house: the comfiest armchair by the window, tea and cake, a garden showing off, a view from the porch — and a knowing wink that the judge has no shoes, no coat, and no need of either. Come Sunday at four; stay for the sunset. Drawn from scratch with Pillow, deterministic, no network.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` draws the invitation and writes `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MiB). `draw_letter.py` is the same drawing script, shipped for reproducibility.
